    Questions people ask

    Will customers know they're talking to an AI?

    Yes. Agents introduce themselves as your assistant, and customers can ask for a person at any time.

    What if an agent doesn't know the answer?

    It says so and passes the conversation to you with a short summary, so you never start from scratch.

    Can I control what gets sent automatically?

    Yes. You choose which kinds of replies are sent on their own and which wait for your approval.

    Can I cancel whenever I want?

    Yes. Plans are month to month, with no cancellation fees.
